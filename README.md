# Meg Schmidt

### A multidisciplinary thinker exploring ideas, language, art, and code.

I'm an independent creative interested in the connections between how we think, what we make, and how we understand the world.

My work moves between visual art, design, typography, language, technology, and experimentation. I'm particularly interested in exploring ideas at their roots and turning abstract concepts into tangible creative experiences.

### Areas of Interest

- Art, graphic design, and illustration
- Typography, language, and etymology
- Creative coding and interactive experiences
- Generative art and artificial intelligence
- Game development and digital storytelling
- Symbolism, philosophy, and systems of thought
- Independent creative projects

### Current Projects

- **[Kitschy Lemon](https://kitschylemon.com)** — Independent art, design, and creative products.
- **[Etymon.guru](https://etymon.guru)** — Exploring language, etymology, and the origins of meaning.
- **Asha Being** — An independent exploration of game development and interactive experiences.

### Tools & Technologies

Adobe Illustrator · Photoshop · After Effects · Inkscape · Godot · Shopify · AI-assisted creative tools

### Philosophy

I'm interested in the space where creativity meets understanding.

I believe making things is a way of thinking, language is a way of exploring ideas, and technology is another medium through which human creativity can express itself.

Always learning. Always exploring. Always making.
